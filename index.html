Learning objectives

After studying these lessons, you should be able to:

Explain ICT

Define ICT and name its three components: hardware, software, and the internet.

Compare websites

Tell static from dynamic websites and identify at least ten website types.

Explore careers

Describe the roles of programmers, web developers, designers, animators, cybersecurity staff, and data analysts.

Practice digital citizenship

Apply netiquette, respond to cyberbullying, and protect your accounts from cyber threats.

Teacher tip

Use the Lessons for discussion, then assign the quiz and group tasks in Activities.
